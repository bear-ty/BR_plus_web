Abstract

Internet human videos contain rich demonstrations of everyday behavior, but converting them into executable robot motion at scale remains challenging. BeyondRetarget Plus presents a scalable framework for collecting robot-ready motion data directly from massive in-the-wild human videos. The framework combines visual motion understanding, robot-aware adaptation, temporal consistency, and contact-aware refinement to transform unconstrained video observations into physically plausible and executable robot trajectories. By supporting diverse humanoid embodiments and reducing the need for costly motion-capture collection, BeyondRetarget Plus provides a practical path toward large-scale robot motion data construction from Internet videos.

Failure Case

Camera motion estimation may struggle with videos featuring overly uniform backgrounds, potentially leading to abnormal trajectories. Highly dynamic and challenging motions may cause estimation failures due to motion blur and out-of-distribution (OOD) inputs.
